Endo Workspace

Endo adds a tooth-region source review workflow on Mac, iPhone, and iPad. It uses the STSR26 Dataset513 PulpSegPretrain checkpoint requested for this workspace. The checkpoint predicts whole-tooth and pulp regions independently. TIPs is not part of this implementation.

Workflow

  1. Open the original DICOM study and choose Endo (Command-5 on Mac).
  2. Align the crosshairs on the tooth, set the target, and enter a tooth identifier. Adjust the region to include the tooth. The target is manually localized; the app does not assign or verify a tooth number.
  3. Run Segment Tooth & Pulp. The bundled Core ML model runs locally.
  4. Compare the colored masks with the acquired axial, coronal, and sagittal slices. Optional Super Resolution and Metal Artifact Reduction apply while navigating; Show Original compares the same acquired slice instantly. Painting, erasing, or placing canal points displays Original · editing and uses the acquired single slice. Paint or erase masks as needed; undo and restoration of the original prediction are available. The 3D view shows tooth and pulp independently.
  5. Record manual canal paths, a reference landmark, and review notes. A path's length is the sum of straight segments between the placed 3D points in millimetres. It is not an established working length.
  6. Review the current result and export separate tooth and pulp STL objects with a JSON record of geometry, model provenance, edits, traces, and notes.
  7. Use Save State or Save State Copy to retain the workspace. Reopening requires the matching original scan. Export approval is never restored.

Replacing a target or region with existing masks or traces requires explicit confirmation. Mask edits and changes to recorded information invalidate export review. Closing or replacing a study cancels pending work.

Paint and erase sweep the selected physical-radius brush between accepted pointer samples on the same acquired slice. Unequal column, row and slice spacing is respected, the stroke is clipped to the target region, and one stroke produces one undo step. Erasing pulp returns those voxels to tooth; erasing tooth preserves pulp. Samples from different source planes or slices are never connected.

A change to the source, slice, target region, tool or brush settings before release discards the pending gesture, even if the controls are later returned to their original values. A gesture exceeding the point limit is also discarded as a whole; release and start a shorter stroke. No retained prefix is joined to the final release point after intermediate samples were dropped.

Wide windows show the inspector beside all four views. On iPhone and narrow windows, use the Axial / Coronal / Sagittal / 3D picker for the full-width view and Controls for target, trace, and review settings. On iPhone and iPad, backgrounding cancels pending inference at the next prediction boundary; returning to the same study starts it again from the beginning after the cancelled work has drained. Explicit cancellation and changing studies discard that pending restart.

Model and Geometry

  • Upstream: STSR26, commit f2af8d41cb1d1760ce9009befa0117f2ed4706b0.
  • Checkpoint SHA-256: 13180dc59f3e28cd0fabe8b07947abf5052a9f6d8cac327a8802adf986a8d094.
  • Single-tooth stage: Dataset513_PulpSegPretrain, 3d_fullres, fold_all, checkpoint_best.pth; 0.3 mm model sampling, YZX patch shape 56 x 88 x 48.
  • User localization replaces the upstream jaw and numbered-tooth cascade. This is an adaptation of the single-tooth stage, not the complete 64-class pipeline. Neighboring teeth can be included and require manual review.
  • Native preprocessing adds 2 mm context where scan coverage allows, resamples in canonical LPS coordinates, normalizes the crop, and blends overlapping model logits. Predictions map back to the original source ROI.
  • Every tile uses nnU-Net's mirrored test-time augmentation. The model runs on the tile and its seven mirrored copies, covering every combination of the three axes the checkpoint's default trainer allows. Each output is flipped back, and the logits are averaged before blending. This is always on and costs about eight times the single-pass model time. On October 12 on this Mac, three teeth in one example scan took 18–24 s with the default 24 mm region, compared with 2.4–3.2 s single-pass. The 36 mm maximum region took about 75 s. Mirroring reduced the tooth and pulp predicted outside the target tooth, and pulp inside the target tooth changed little. It does not establish accuracy (the app repository's Tools/Endodontics/README.md records the model notes). The upstream STSR26 script runs this stage with --disable_tta, so the app follows nnU-Net's default predictor instead.
  • Every editing pane uses one acquired source slice, independent of MPR slab thickness or image-enhancement previews. Navigation previews use a separate display layer; segmentation, mask edits, traces, 3D surfaces, and exports retain source geometry and source-based masks. Original DICOM pixels are unchanged.
  • Mesh coordinates are millimetres in the DICOM patient LPS frame. Native mask sampling, crop boundaries, and source geometry remain part of the export.
  • Tooth and pulp surfaces follow the Surface Export Surface setting, and the 3D pane and the export always use the same one. Smooth (the default) smooths and simplifies each surface, puts back local shrinkage and caps each piece's volume change at 10%. Vertices move at most 0.25 mm from the unsmoothed extracted surface, plus at most about 0.03 mm from simplification; because that extracted surface already cuts voxel corners, the result can lie about 0.3 mm (0.15 mm voxels) to 0.4 mm (0.4 mm voxels) from the voxel-mask boundary. No piece is removed or bridged; the tooth's enclosed voids are filled (they have no surface), and the masks are unchanged. Exact voxels keeps the unsmoothed source-grid surface. Changing the setting clears the Endo surfaces and the Endo review; the surfaces are rebuilt at once in Endo, or when Endo is next opened. Long enclosed-void traversals check cancellation within the flood, so closing, replacement and cancellation can stop that stage before it finishes. endodontic-summary.json (schema 3) records the style and each surface's settings and triangle counts. Its model block and the Endo saved state (schema 2) record mirroredInference, which is true for every app run. Endo saved states from earlier versions are rejected as invalid instead of being migrated.
  • Numerical conversion checks compare Core ML against the original PyTorch checkpoint. They do not measure anatomical accuracy.

The reproducible converter is Tools/Endodontics/convert_stsr26.py. Python is development tooling only. The app uses its bundled model without a Python install, server, account, or network access.

Interpretation and Privacy

No expert reference masks were supplied for the example studies. Predicted chambers, fine branches, and apical or accessory canals can be incomplete or incorrect. Metal, motion, scan resolution, and cropping affect the result. Disconnected pieces are not a canal count, and a missing prediction does not establish absence of a canal. A smaller output voxel pitch cannot create anatomical information absent from the source.

Pulp predicted outside the independent tooth region is preserved and reported for review. Pulp contacting the ROI boundary is flagged because exported crop surfaces can be artificially closed. No automatic bridging or invented canal completion is performed.

Saved states and reviews contain Endo masks and user-authored text as well as sensitive anatomical geometry. Text can identify a person. Endo exports do not automatically copy DICOM demographics, but they are not anonymized records. The workspace is experimental and has not been validated for diagnosis, access preparation, or treatment decisions. Upstream's MIT license is preserved; its README also describes research and academic use.

Verification

The October 14 audit added synthetic regressions for continuous paint/erase, anisotropic brush radius, ROI clipping, plane changes, grouped undo and cancellation during enclosed-void filling. The complete Mac suite passed 1,022 tests with 21 optional-input skips; the iPhone iOS 18.6 Simulator suite passed 1,018 with 14 skips; targeted iPad simulator checks passed 148 with no skips. All had zero failures. These checks do not establish the feel of the final brush gesture on a physical device or clinical accuracy. No private study was opened or enabled for this audit.

The app repository's STATUS.md and docs/AUDIT_COVERAGE.tsv record the exact candidate, verification boundaries and per-file review. Its dated October 9 Endo validation record preserves earlier native tests, example-scan checks, visual inspection and limitations; those private-study results were not rerun by the October 14 audit.
