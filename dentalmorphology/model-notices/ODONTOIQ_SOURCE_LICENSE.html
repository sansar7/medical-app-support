tooth-scan.glb — a real human tooth from a micro-CT scan

Source: ds-uct-002 "Root Canal Strain: X-Ray micro-CT of four teeth before and after
root canal procedure", scan tomo1b (a tooth before any treatment, 20 µm voxels).
  Pereira, A., Tonelli, S., Antunes, M. A., Miranda, A., Silveira, F. F. (2020).
  Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3877625

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made for OdontoIQ (scripts/build-tooth-scan.ts): the scan was read at
40 µm, separated into enamel, dentin and pulp by density (cementum can't be told
apart from dentin in this scan, so it is included with the dentin), converted to
smoothed surfaces, stood upright and centred, simplified, and compressed into one
glTF file. It is shown on OdontoIQ, a student project, for illustration only.
