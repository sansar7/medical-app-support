The dataset records no tooth type, number or side. ToothForm assigned each
tooth's class and side from our in-house morphology review (three independent
visual analyses plus a morphometric script, then a consensus; for 0039, crown,
root and canal morphometrics with CT sections, then the owner's review of the
rendered views); this is an inference, not a source label and not
expert-confirmed. The side is the side
as imaged, because the scans' image handedness is not documented. Each mirrored
position is a reflected copy of the same tooth with reversed triangle winding,
not another specimen. The teeth are six different specimens, not a matched set.

Changes made by ToothForm: enamel, dentin and pulp were segmented automatically
from the published volumes on their native 72 micrometre grid (intensity
thresholds, seeded watershed and partial-volume boundary labelling). Pulp
includes canal stretches traced only where the voxels are measurably darker
than the surrounding dentin (for 0059, sub-voxel apical canals are drawn at a
0.216 mm minimum width; for 0056, 6,587 scan-hard voxels were relabelled as
pulp this way); tiny enclosed voids were filled; small label refinements are
listed per tooth in the app's model notes. Labels were
converted to millimetre surface meshes, placed by one proper rotation and
translation per tooth, simplified with meshoptimizer for display tiers, and
given smoothed display normals (shading only). For tooth 0056 the surfaces
were also smoothed with every vertex kept within half a voxel. No crown repair,
surface patching, idealised anatomy or nonuniform scaling was added. The labels
are not expert annotations and make no clinical claim. Tissue colours and
translucency are illustrative display colours.
