Demo 1001: Micro-CT tooth, Tomo1B (tooth class and side unspecified)
Original source: Pereira, Andre; Tonelli, Stéphanie; Antunes, Marcelo Avelar;
Miranda, Antonio; Silveira, Frank Ferreira (2020). ds-uct-002: Root Canal Strain:
X-Ray micro-CT of four teeth before and after root canal procedure.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3877625
Original scan: Zeiss Xradia Versa 510, nominal 20 µm voxels, before treatment.
Public derivative by OdontoIQ:
https://odontoliq.com/models/tooth-scan.glb
https://odontoliq.com/models/tooth-scan-LICENSE.txt
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
See ODONTOIQ_SOURCE_LICENSE.txt for the supplied derivative license notice.

OdontoIQ read the source at 40 µm sampling, separated enamel, dentin (including
indistinguishable cementum), and pulp by density, smoothed and simplified the
surfaces, and normalized pose. ToothForm decoded the public GLB, retained
all triangles, applied one shared 180-degree X rotation and uniform display
scale of 5 to all layers, exported OBJ, and compiled native SceneKit geometry.
Display units are not calibrated anatomical measurements. Source segmentation
parameters are not supplied. The source enamel has an open/nonmanifold region;
small surface irregularities may reflect anatomy, scanning, or segmentation.
No additional canal or chamber geometry was fabricated.

Demo 1103: RCAP maxillary first molar (side unspecified)
Demo 1108: RCAP maxillary central incisor (side unspecified)
Source: Prof. Marco A. Versiani / The Root Canal Anatomy Project
https://rootcanalanatomy.blogspot.com/2013/06/a-new-microct-based-educational-resource.html
Original VXM files: 062_Max_1st_Molar_Versiani.vxm and
062_Max_Central_Incisor_Versiani.vxm, distributed through the project's links.
Source terms allow attributed noncommercial educational use. These assets are
included only in this local educational prototype; commercial/App Store
redistribution requires appropriate permission. No standardized Creative
Commons license is asserted for these RCAP files.

ToothForm decoded the highest-resolution supplied VXM volume and derived
hard tissue using a 60/255 threshold plus the largest component. The candidate
pulp is the largest connected internal void from axial hole filling; other
voids were excluded. Both masks were smoothed with Gaussian sigma 0.6 voxels and
converted to a 0.5 isosurface with one shared rigid coordinate conversion. No
canal connectors, synthetic pulp horns, or nonuniform scale were introduced.
The mobile export voxel spacings are approximately 91.573 µm isotropic for the
molar and 45.786 × 45.786 × 91.573 µm for the incisor; original scanner acquisition
resolution is unspecified. Original export dimensions are retained.

Axial hole filling stops when a void communicates with the slice exterior.
Zero padding and the isosurface create finite canal end caps. Apical foramina,
accessory openings, and disconnected spaces are not asserted preserved.
Automatic segmentations were visually checked against source slices; this does
not establish clinical accuracy or diagnostic suitability.
