Dental Scope selected and simplified BodyParts3D tooth meshes, added modeled
tooth-specific crown relief and internal anatomy, and derived the third molars
from neighboring second molars. The pulp chambers and canals are schematic;
they were not segmented from medical images. These surfaces illustrate one
configuration and do not represent prevalence, apical anatomy, or a patient.

ToothForm modifications: meshopt decompression, moderate triangle
simplification (relative target error 0.0006), rigid per-tooth orientation,
conversion from centimeters to millimeters, translation, recomputed smooth
normals and Wavefront OBJ export. Schematic overlapping coronal connector
surfaces were added to 54 canals whose source surfaces ended short of the pulp
chamber, so the educational display shows continuity. These are visual model
adaptations, not measured canal anatomy. No mirrored teeth or scanned canals
were added to this model family. Per-file hashes, transformations and connector
metadata are preserved in manifest.json.
