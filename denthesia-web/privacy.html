Denthesia website privacy policy

Updated September 29, 2026.

Patient entries, combined-dose drafts, and helper-session timing stay in the current tab’s temporary browser storage. The application does not send these values to a server. Starting a new patient clears those drafts. Closing a tab usually clears them, but browser session recovery may retain them. Office inventory, notes, concentration presets, and appearance are stored on the current device. Avoid patient identifiers in office notes or presets.

Language selection uses the browser’s ordered device-language preferences. It does not use geolocation, a language cookie, or a stored language override. Translations are bundled with the application and its offline copy; the website makes no translation API requests, downloads no translation models, and does not submit patient values, notes, or preset names for translation. Developer-side drafting operates on public app-authored source text only. Russian resources are not included; Vietnamese and the other supported languages are documented in language coverage. Switching languages does not erase or translate stored user content.

There is no application analytics, shared patient record, or cross-device synchronization. Hosting and any site sign-in may process ordinary request and account information. External product-label and reference links have their own privacy practices. Browser storage is not a protected medical record and does not provide the native app’s encrypted storage or biometric lock.

Support email is optional and is sent only when you choose to send it. Do not include patient information. Contact sansargupta10@gmail.com for software support or privacy questions.
